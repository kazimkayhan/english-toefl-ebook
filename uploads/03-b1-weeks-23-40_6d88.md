# Stage 3 — B1 (Weeks 23–40)

Narrative tenses, used to, present perfect continuous, second conditional, passives, deduction modals, gerunds/infinitives, cohesion, indirect questions, skills for reading/listening/writing/speaking. B1 gate at Week 40.

## Week 23 — Narrative pasts: past continuous
**Level band:** B1  
**Grammar focus:** past continuous + when/while  
**Weekly pronunciation:** integrate sounds/stress/linking in warm-ups; Day 6 includes a short pronunciation recycle.  
**Study load:** ~12 hrs/week across Days 1–6 (Day 6 = review/integration heavier).

### Week 23 · Day 1 — was/were + -ing
**Level:** B1  
**Time:** ~100–120 min  
**Goal:** Learn and use 'past continuous + when/while' accurately while expanding vocabulary for 'was/were + -ing'.

**1. Warm-up (10–15 min)**  
- Re-read yesterday's checkpoint and mark yes/no honestly.
- Two-minute free speak on: 'was/were + -ing' (phone voice memo).
- Quick Anki: 10–15 due cards (new + review).
- Activate prior knowledge: list five words you already know related to today's topic.

**2. Learn (25–35 min)**  
- Grammar/point: past continuous + when/while
- Vocab (12 items listed): lecture; assignment; exam; grade; tuition; campus; degree; semester; scholarship; research; tutor; syllabus
- Examples (3–5 full sentences):

  - I used to live near the campus, but now I rent an apartment downtown.
  - I have been working on this project for three weeks.
  - If I had more free time, I would volunteer at the library.
  - The results were published last year; the method is still used today.

**3. Practice (20–25 min)**  
- Controlled drill: ten gap-fills on (past continuous + when/while).
- Matching: match eight vocab items to definitions or pictures; then cover and recall.
