Irregular pasts, comparatives, quantifiers, futures, present perfect intro, modals of advice/obligation, first conditional, basic relatives, reported speech intro. A2 gate at Week 22.

## Week 11 — Past simple irregulars
**Level band:** A2  
**Grammar focus:** Common irregular past forms  
**Weekly pronunciation:** integrate sounds/stress/linking in warm-ups; Day 6 includes a short pronunciation recycle.  
**Study load:** ~12 hrs/week across Days 1–6 (Day 6 = review/integration heavier).

### Week 11 · Day 1 — High-frequency irregulars
**Level:** A2  
**Time:** ~90–120 min  
**Goal:** Learn and use 'Common irregular past forms' accurately while expanding vocabulary for 'High-frequency irregulars'.

**1. Warm-up (10–15 min)**  
- Re-read yesterday's checkpoint and mark yes/no honestly.
- Two-minute free speak on: 'High-frequency irregulars' (phone voice memo).
- Quick Anki: 10–15 due cards (new + review).
- Pronunciation: one minute of word-stress tapping on today's new vocab.

**2. Learn (25–35 min)**  
- Grammar/point: Common irregular past forms
- Vocab (12 items listed): price; cheap; expensive; discount; receipt; cash; credit card; size; fit; try on; change; customer
- Examples (3–5 full sentences):
  - I went to the market and bought price and cheap.
  - This city is bigger and more expensive than my hometown.
  - We are going to travel next month if we get tickets.
  - I think it will rain later; look at those clouds.

**3. Practice (20–25 min)**  
- Controlled drill: ten gap-fills on (Common irregular past forms).
- Matching: match eight vocab items to definitions or pictures; then cover and recall.
- Sentence transformation: rewrite six sentences changing tense, aspect, or voice as required by today's point.
- Dictation: write four sentences from slow audio (self-record, then play back) focusing on today's forms.
- Cloze paragraph (80–120 words) targeting today's grammar and six vocab items.

**4. Produce (20–25 min)**  
- Speak: Record a voice memo on 'High-frequency irregulars'. Listen once and note three improvements. Target: 60–90 seconds; include five target vocab items and three target grammar forms.
- Write: Write 100–130 words on 'High-frequency irregulars'. Include connectors (and/but/because/so).

**5. Review (10–15 min)**  
- Anki: add today's new vocab (price, cheap, expensive, discount, receipt, cash…). Tags: Week11-Day1.
- Anki: grammar pattern card — one model sentence plus cloze deletion of the target form.
- Re-speak the hardest sentence from Produce until smooth (maximum three attempts).

**Checkpoint:** Without looking, write five vocab items plus three example sentences using today's grammar. Check accuracy. Pass if at least four vocab and two sentences are correct. Yes/No.
