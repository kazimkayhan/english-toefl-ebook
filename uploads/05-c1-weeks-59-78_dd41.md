### Week 59 · Day 1 — Complex subordination
**Level:** C1  
**Time:** ~110–120 min  
**Goal:** Learn and use 'subordination density; absolute clauses intro' accurately while expanding vocabulary for 'Complex subordination'.

**1. Warm-up (10–15 min)**  
- Re-read yesterday's checkpoint and mark yes/no honestly.
- Two-minute free speak on: 'Complex subordination' (phone voice memo).
- Quick Anki: 10–15 due cards (new + review).
- Activate prior knowledge: list five words you already know related to today's topic.

**2. Learn (25–35 min)**  
- Grammar/point: subordination density; absolute clauses intro
- Vocab (14 items listed): moreover; however; therefore; in contrast; for instance; in other words; as a result; on the other hand; specifically; broadly speaking; to some extent; ultimately; build a sentence; email
- Examples (3–5 full sentences):
  - Furthermore, the study constitutes a useful approach to assessing learner progress.
  - When paraphrasing, retain the original meaning while changing structure and lexis.
  - Listen and Repeat rewards clear stress, accurate vowels, and natural linking.
  - In Academic Discussion posts, state a position, support it, and engage another view.

**3. Practice (20–25 min)**  
- Controlled drill: ten gap-fills on (subordination density; absolute clauses intro).
- Matching: match eight vocab items to definitions or pictures; then cover and recall.
- Sentence transformation: rewrite six sentences changing tense, aspect, or voice as required by today's point.
- Dictation: write four sentences from slow audio (self-record, then play back) focusing on today's forms.
- Cloze paragraph (80–120 words) targeting today's grammar and six vocab items.
- Timed accuracy set: eight items in six minutes; mark and classify errors (form vs meaning).
- Paraphrase set: rewrite five sentences preserving meaning with denser or more academic wording.

**4. Produce (20–25 min)**  
- Speak: Record a voice memo on 'Complex subordination'. Listen once and note three improvements. Target: Interview-style three questions (30–45 sec each); then one Listen-and-Repeat line three times.
- Write: Academic Discussion-style response (150–200 words) OR formal email (120–160) on 'Complex subordination'. Self-check task fulfillment.

**5. Review (10–15 min)**  
- Anki: add today's new vocab (moreover, however, therefore, in contrast, for instance, in other words…). Tags: Week59-Day1.
- Anki: grammar pattern card — one model sentence plus cloze deletion of the target form.
- Re-speak the hardest sentence from Produce until smooth (maximum three attempts).
