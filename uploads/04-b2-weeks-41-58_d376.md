## Week 41 — Narrative tenses mastery
**Level band:** B2  
**Grammar focus:** past simple/continuous/perfect  
**Weekly pronunciation:** integrate sounds/stress/linking in warm-ups; Day 6 includes a short pronunciation recycle.  
**Study load:** ~12 hrs/week across Days 1–6 (Day 6 = review/integration heavier).

### Week 41 · Day 1 — Past perfect form
**Level:** B2  
**Time:** ~100–120 min  
**Goal:** Learn and use 'past simple/continuous/perfect' accurately while expanding vocabulary for 'Past perfect form'.

**1. Warm-up (10–15 min)**  
- Re-read yesterday's checkpoint and mark yes/no honestly.
- Two-minute free speak on: 'Past perfect form' (phone voice memo).
- Quick Anki: 10–15 due cards (new + review).
- Activate prior knowledge: list five words you already know related to today's topic.

**2. Learn (25–35 min)**  
- Grammar/point: past simple/continuous/perfect
- Vocab (14 items listed): approach; area; assess; assume; authority; available; benefit; concept; consist; constitute; context; contract; create; data
- Examples (3–5 full sentences):
  - By the time we arrived, the speaker had already finished the introduction.
  - If she had checked the deadline, she would not have missed the submission.
  - What matters most is clear evidence, not confident speculation.
  - Having reviewed the data, the team revised its initial hypothesis.

**3. Practice (20–25 min)**  
- Controlled drill: ten gap-fills on (past simple/continuous/perfect).
- Matching: match eight vocab items to definitions or pictures; then cover and recall.
- Sentence transformation: rewrite six sentences changing tense, aspect, or voice as required by today's point.
- Dictation: write four sentences from slow audio (self-record, then play back) focusing on today's forms.
- Cloze paragraph (80–120 words) targeting today's grammar and six vocab items.
- Timed accuracy set: eight items in six minutes; mark and classify errors (form vs meaning).
- Paraphrase set: rewrite five sentences preserving meaning with denser or more academic wording.

**4. Produce (20–25 min)**  
- Speak: Record a voice memo on 'Past perfect form'. Listen once and note three improvements. Target: 2–3 minutes; use discourse markers and one paraphrase of a key idea.
- Write: Write 180–220 words on 'Past perfect form' in a semi-formal register; include one hedge and one contrast connector.

**5. Review (10–15 min)**  
- Anki: add today's new vocab (approach, area, assess, assume, authority, available…). Tags: Week41-Day1.
- Anki: grammar pattern card — one model sentence plus cloze deletion of the target form.
- Re-speak the hardest sentence from Produce until smooth (maximum three attempts).

**Checkpoint:** Without looking, write five vocab items plus three example sentences using today's grammar. Check accuracy. Pass if at least four vocab and two sentences are correct. Yes/No.
