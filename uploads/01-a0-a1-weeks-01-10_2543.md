# Stage 1 — A0 → A1 (Weeks 1–10)

Absolute beginner to elementary. Heavy phonics, be-verb, present simple, past of be, past simple regular. Pronunciation every week.

## Week 1 — Alphabet, sounds, greetings
**Level band:** A0/A1  
**Grammar focus:** be (I am / you are); alphabet & phonics  
**Weekly pronunciation:** integrate sounds/stress/linking in warm-ups; Day 6 includes a short pronunciation recycle.  
**Study load:** ~12 hrs/week across Days 1–6 (Day 6 = review/integration heavier).

### Week 1 · Day 1 — Alphabet & letter sounds
**Level:** A0/A1  
**Time:** ~90–120 min  
**Goal:** Learn and use 'be (I am / you are); alphabet & phonics' accurately while expanding vocabulary for 'Alphabet & letter sounds'.

**1. Warm-up (10–15 min)**  
- Re-read yesterday's checkpoint and mark yes/no honestly.
- Two-minute free speak on: 'Alphabet & letter sounds' (phone voice memo).
- Quick Anki: 10–15 due cards (new + review).
- Phonemes: shadow five minimal pairs or alphabet chant (day focus sound).

**2. Learn (25–35 min)**  
- Grammar/point: be (I am / you are); alphabet & phonics
- Pronunciation: focus on clear /iː/ vs /ɪ/, final -s, and word stress on new nouns.
- Vocab (10 items listed): one; two; three; four; five; six; seven; eight; nine; ten
- Examples (3–5 full sentences):
  - I am a student. I am two.
  - This is my one. It is new.
  - Are you from this city? Yes, I am. / No, I am not.
  - She is a teacher. He is an engineer.

**3. Practice (20–25 min)**  
- Controlled drill: ten gap-fills on (be (I am / you are); alphabet & phonics).
- Matching: match eight vocab items to definitions or pictures; then cover and recall.
- Sentence transformation: rewrite six sentences changing tense, aspect, or voice as required by today's point.
- Dictation: write four sentences from slow audio (self-record, then play back) focusing on today's forms.

**4. Produce (20–25 min)**  
- Speak: Record a voice memo on 'Alphabet & letter sounds'. Listen once and note three improvements. Target: 45–60 seconds; speak slowly with clear final consonants.
- Write: Write 4–6 sentences about 'Alphabet & letter sounds'. Check be/present forms. (about 40–60 words)

**5. Review (10–15 min)**  
- Anki: add today's new vocab (one, two, three, four, five, six…). Tags: Week1-Day1.
- Anki: grammar pattern card — one model sentence plus cloze deletion of the target form.
- Re-speak the hardest sentence from Produce until smooth (maximum three attempts).

**Checkpoint:** Without looking, write five vocab items plus three example sentences using today's grammar. Check accuracy. Pass if at least four vocab and two sentences are correct. Yes/No.
